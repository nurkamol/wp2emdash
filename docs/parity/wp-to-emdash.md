# WordPress → EmDash parity

What a small-business or content WordPress site can contain, where each thing lives in EmDash 1.5.0, what the kit does with it today, and what closing each gap takes. The same data is in [`wp-to-emdash.json`](./wp-to-emdash.json); keep the two in sync.

As of 2026-11-20, against EmDash 1.5.0 (installed source) and the kit at that date. Evidence from real sites is counted across the two migrated sites only, with no client details. Status values: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ❌ missing · ➖ superseded (the platform replaces the WordPress feature, so nothing has to be carried) · ⛔ out of scope. Effort: S ≤ 1 day, M = 2–5 days, L = 1–3 weeks.

## 1. Executive summary

The matrix covers 110 features; 103 of them are in scope.

| | Count | Share of in-scope |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Done | 74 | 72% |
| ➖ Superseded (nothing to carry) | 6 | 6% |
| 🟡 Partial | 19 | 18% |
| ❌ Missing | 4 | 4% |
| ⛔ Out of scope (not counted) | 7 | — |

**What this means.** The kit fully migrates a brochure site: builder pages (WPBakery/Salient, Bricks, Gutenberg pages; Elementor, Divi and the Gutenberg block libraries since 2026-11-21, not yet on a real site), header and footer, menus, popups and announcement bars, media in use, forms, SEO meta, JSON-LD, analytics, redirects from plugin exports, and old upload/sitemap/`?page_id` URLs. Both real sites were exactly that. They had no posts, comments, CPT entries, terms in use, widgets, consent banner or redirect rules. Since 2026-11-20 the kit also migrates a blog: posts, categories and tags, bylines, archives and RSS feeds (tested on a synthetic WordPress blog and a local EmDash, not yet on a real blog). Since 2026-11-21 custom post types become their own collections with their ACF, Meta Box and Pods fields, archives and term archives (tested the same way).

Anything beyond that is missing today. Since 2026-11-21 approved comments are migrated too, with their date, author and threads (synthetic test). Since 2026-11-21 the site features are carried too: every menu, classic widget areas, a search page, breadcrumbs, the old 404 page. The kit has no adapters for other builders (the Elementor and Divi adapters exist since 2026-11-21 but have not met a real site yet); a built-in consent banner exists since 2026-11-20. Since 2026-11-20 none of it is lost silently: transform lists every unmigrated post type, taxonomy in use and author (gate `content-types-handled`), Customizer CSS is reported and carried by decision, and verify accounts for every old address (`urls-accounted`): feeds, search, archives, attachment pages and query URLs follow a per-site rule set.

**Top 10 gaps by impact**

1. **~~Blog posts are not migrated.~~** Closed 2026-11-20 (blog milestone). Rows: posts, post-dates, post-permalinks, excerpt, featured-image.
2. **~~Verify ignores audited URLs that are not migrated pages.~~** Closed 2026-11-20: verify `urls-accounted` checks every audited, discovered and WordPress-form URL. Row: archive-urls.
3. **~~Custom post types are not migrated.~~** Closed 2026-11-21 (CPT mapper): a collection per public type with its ACF, Meta Box and Pods fields, archives and term archives. Rows: cpt, acf.
4. **~~Categories, tags and custom taxonomies are extracted but never loaded.~~** Closed 2026-11-20 for posts and 2026-11-21 for custom post types. Rows: categories-tags, custom-taxonomies.
5. **~~No authors or bylines.~~** Closed 2026-11-20 (guest bylines). Row: bylines.
6. **~~The starter has no search page.~~** Closed 2026-11-21: `/search/?q=` with EmDash full-text search; `?s=` goes there. Row: search.
7. **~~No RSS feeds.~~** Closed 2026-11-20: blog sites serve RSS 2.0 site and archive feeds. Row: rss.
8. **~~Comments are extracted but not loaded.~~** Closed 2026-11-21: approved comments with date, author and threads through an admin-only import route, approved by EmDash's moderation. Row: comments.
9. **Third-party scripts now have a home.** The snippet recognizer, the 46-provider registry, site widgets and the built-in consent banner carry them; unknown code still waits for a decision (gate `site-snippets`).
10. **Five builders have adapters.** WPBakery/Salient, Gutenberg (with Kadence, Spectra, GenerateBlocks, Stackable and CoBlocks blocks), Bricks and (since 2026-11-21, unmeasured on a real site) Elementor and Divi. Pages of any other builder or theme go through the DOM fallback (2026-11-21, unmeasured on a real site): rebuilt from the live page's rendered HTML, flagged, and held at the transform gate until the owner has checked each one. Rows: elementor, divi, gutenberg-libs, other-builders.
